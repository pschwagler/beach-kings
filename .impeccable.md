## Design Context

### Users

Beach volleyball players use Beach League to organize league and pickup play,
record scores, manage sessions, and understand rankings and performance. Mobile
flows should be quick to scan and operate during active play.

### Brand Personality

Sporting, confident, and straightforward. The interface should feel organized
and energetic without becoming loud, decorative, or corporate.

### Aesthetic Direction

Follow the checked-in mobile wireframes and their token system: strong teal
navigation, gold primary actions, restrained semantic color, clear iOS-style
type hierarchy, compact cards, and generous touch targets. Support light and
dark themes through semantic tokens. Treat the wireframes as the structural
source of truth while correcting documented usability problems rather than
copying them literally.

### Design Principles

1. Make the next action obvious through a consistent teal/gold hierarchy.
2. Reserve gray and reduced emphasis for genuinely unavailable controls.
3. Distinguish information from inputs; read-only values should not look
   editable.
4. Prefer compact, scannable layouts suited to courtside use.
5. Use semantic theme tokens and established components instead of one-off
   colors or patterns.
